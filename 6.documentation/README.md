\# E-Commerce Sales Analytics \& AI-Assisted Business Insights



\## Project Overview



An end-to-end e-commerce sales analytics project built to transform raw sales data into interactive business insights using Excel, MySQL, Power BI, DAX, and Generative AI.



\## Tools Used



\* Excel

\* MySQL

\* Power BI

\* DAX

\* Generative AI



\## Project Workflow



Raw Data

→ Excel Data Cleaning

→ Data Modeling

→ MySQL Database

→ Power BI

→ DAX Measures

→ Interactive Dashboard

→ AI-Assisted Business Insights



\## Key Metrics



\* Total Sales: ₹144.69M

\* Total Profit: ₹21.60M

\* Total Orders: 1,200

\* Total Customers: 220

\* Profit Margin: 14.93%



\## Key Business Insights



\* Furniture is the top category.

\* Binders is the top reported product.

\* Kerala is the top-performing state.

\* Corporate is the strongest customer segment.

\* Total profit is ₹21.60M with a reported profit margin of 14.93%.



\## Dashboard Features



The Power BI dashboard includes:



\* Sales performance analysis

\* Profitability analysis

\* Product and category analysis

\* Customer segment analysis

\* Geographic analysis

\* KPI cards

\* Interactive slicers

\* DAX measures

\* AI-assisted business insights



\## AI-Assisted Analysis



A dedicated AI Business Insights page was created to interpret the dashboard results and provide business recommendations.



The AI analysis was used as a supporting analytical layer based on the available dashboard results.



\## Business Recommendations



1\. Prioritize deeper analysis of high-performing categories, customer segments, and geographic markets.

2\. Investigate Binders performance across sales, profitability, pricing, and customer distribution.

3\. Analyze profitability by product, category, customer segment, and state to identify opportunities for improvement.
